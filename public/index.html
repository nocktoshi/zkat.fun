Why the name? +

ZK is the zero-knowledge reference. Kat is the punchline. Nockchain’s miners produce proofs of computation; our cat appears to have no computation to prove.

$ZKat is a Nockchain-inspired memecoin on Base, with NOCK rewards reported by BaseStonk. The privacy theme reflects Nockchain’s vision: its current docs say private transactions are still in development, and mining proofs do not hide transaction values or chain state.
