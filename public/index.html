How the tax works +

The tax is taken inside the Uniswap v4 swap on Base, so it applies when $ZKAT is bought or sold. Ordinary transfers do not pay it. Pair-side fees are paid in NOCK. Token-side fees are paid in ZKAT.

Holders receive those assets automatically as other people trade. Claiming is optional on BaseStonk and is never required. Rates are set at launch and cannot be changed.

Why the name? +

ZK is the zero-knowledge reference. Kat is the punchline. Nockchain’s miners produce proofs of computation; our cat appears to have no computation to prove.

$ZKat is a Nockchain-inspired memecoin on Base. Holders receive NOCK and ZKAT from the trading tax, as reported by BaseStonk. The privacy theme reflects Nockchain’s vision: its current docs say private transactions are still in development, and mining proofs do not hide transaction values or chain state.
