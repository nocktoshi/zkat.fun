How $ZKAT pulls the trenches into Nockchain.

$ZKAT · ZERO KNOWLEDGE CAT

Zero-knowledge has spent most of its life in papers. STARKs, Goldilocks fields, proof systems, proving traces. Serious work. Necessary work. And, for almost everyone who actually trades, completely illegible.

Crypto’s other half lives in the trenches: fast charts, worse jokes, tokens that spread because they are funny before they are understood. That half is usually treated as a contaminant. It is also the only distribution engine the industry has ever had that does not require a conference badge.

$ZKAT is a Nockchain-inspired memecoin on Base. It is a cat. It is not Nockchain, not an official product, and not a claim that transfers are anonymous. Private transactions on Nockchain are still in development; mining proofs do not hide values or chain state. The joke is that the cat heard “zero knowledge” and took it personally.

The useful part is what happens when a joke is paired against NOCK.

What you pay, and who gets paid

$ZKAT trades against NOCK. Buys and sells each pay a 1% tax inside the swap. Ordinary transfers do not. The rates were set at launch and cannot be changed.

BaseStonk takes its platform share from that tax, not on top of it. The remainder is paid to holders automatically as people trade. Pair-side fees arrive as NOCK. Token-side fees arrive as ZKAT. There is nothing to farm and nothing you have to claim, though BaseStonk will let you claim early if you want to spend the gas.

That is the whole machine:

  • Traders pay 1% to buy and 1% to sell.
  • Holders receive NOCK and ZKAT from those fees.
  • Volume is the input. The percentage is fixed. Nobody is promising a yield.

The overflowing blue pool on the main page is the live NOCK total BaseStonk reports as paid to holders. The tax panel shows the same feed’s ZKAT total. The extra drip only fires when that NOCK number actually goes up.

So holding $ZKAT is not a story about a team sending you tokens. It is a story about other people trading, and the pool routing a slice of that flow to whoever is sitting still.

The pair is the point

You cannot buy $ZKAT without buying NOCK.

The pool is quoted in NOCK. The chart is NOCK. The rewards are NOCK and ZKAT. If someone in a Telegram trench decides the cat is funny, they do not swipe a random stable and forget the ecosystem. They have to acquire NOCK — the asset Nockchain describes as sound money backed by useful cryptographic work — and then spend it into the pair.

That is a different kind of meme than a dog on a major.

Most memecoins are a closed loop: attention in, attention out, pair against a unit that already has infinite distribution. $ZKAT is an open loop into a proving network. Every new buyer is, at minimum, a new holder of NOCK. Some of them will stop at the joke. Some of them will ask what NOCK is. That question is the product.

Nockchain’s miners do not hash empty puzzles. They generate zero-knowledge proofs of computation. The native currency is hard-capped and minted through that work. Bridged NOCK already trades on Base; that is where this pair lives. $ZKAT does not mine NOCK, does not run on Nockchain, and does not settle ZK-PoW. It does something the proving network is not optimized to do itself: it makes the first click easy.

The flywheel

  1. A cat is easier to share than a whitepaper. The trenches do not forward “a distributed market for verifiable computation.” They forward a cat drooling into a pool of NOCK. That is not a replacement for the paper. It is a door to it.
  2. The door costs NOCK. Demand for $ZKAT is demand for the pair. Buys pull NOCK into the pool. Sells pay holders more NOCK. The meme cannot pump in isolation from the asset it is named after.
  3. Holders get paid to stay. The 1% tax does not go to a marketing wallet. It goes to whoever is holding. That keeps a community sitting in NOCK-denominated exposure instead of flipping out at the first candle. Sitting is how people start reading.
  4. Reading is how the academic layer gets new blood. Someone who bought a cat will, if the joke holds, eventually hit the manifesto sentence: “It’s sound money backed by useful cryptographic work.” From there the path is short: ZK-PoW, proofpower instead of hashpower, NockApps, a proving network that wants to become a market for verifiable computation. None of that starts in a seminar room. It starts because a stranger wanted the cat.
  5. New people make the pair denser, which makes the joke louder. More traders, more tax, more NOCK paid to holders, more screenshots of the pool, more people who now have a reason to care what Nockchain is. That is a flywheel. It is not a guarantee. Volume is the input nobody can promise. The pairing and the tax are what the contracts actually do.

The academic ZK world has spent years trying to explain itself downward. Memes explain upward. They take a dense idea and give it a body that can travel through group chats. If the body is honest about what it is — a meme on Base, inspired by a proving chain, paid in the pair asset — then the people it brings in are not being tricked into the ecosystem. They are being invited in the only language the trenches speak.

Why the trenches belong here

Zero-knowledge culture has a class problem. The people who can read a STARK paper are not the same people who move liquidity at 3 a.m. The first group built the machinery. The second group decides whether anyone outside the machinery ever hears about it.

Bitcoin did not win because the whitepaper was gentle. It won because a cartoon and a number went somewhere the paper could not. Ethereum did not become a home for applications because the Yellow Paper was a beach read. Dogs, frogs, and worse have been the unpaid communications department of crypto for a decade. Pretending that is beneath a ZK chain is how a ZK chain stays a subculture of a subculture.

Nockchain’s bet is civilization-grade: useful proofs as the work that secures money. That bet needs miners, yes. It also needs a public that can say “NOCK” without first surviving a proving-system tutorial. A memetic force is not a substitute for proofpower. It is how proofpower gets a constituency.

$ZKAT is deliberately unserious about the cat and serious about the pair. The tax is public. The contract is public. The vesting is public. The Nockchain docs stay linked, including the parts that say privacy is not here yet. The trenches can have the drool. The ecosystem gets the on-ramp.

What this is not

  • It is not an official Nockchain launch.
  • It is not a promise that $ZKAT lives on Nockchain.
  • It is not anonymous. Base is public. So is the cat.
  • It is not a yield product. Holders earn a slice of tax when people trade; if nobody trades, nobody is paid.
  • It is not a claim that every buyer becomes a miner, a NockApp developer, or a convert to ZK-PoW. Some of them just like cats.

That last point is fine. Ecosystems do not grow by converting only the already-convinced. They grow when a joke is a bridge, and the bridge dumps you onto an asset that actually does the work.
